# Nell — Cousin

**Age:** 5

**Look (locked — never changes across episodes):**
- Two low pigtails with red ties, always
- Purple dress with white polka dots, one favorite stuffed rabbit always somewhere in frame
- Skin tone: warm brown, a shade lighter than Maya's
- Stays over at Maya's often — a recurring, not occasional, presence

**Personality:** Big feelings, no filter yet. Everything is either the best thing ever or the worst thing ever. Carries the "naming feelings" and "what to do when angry" pillars because her feelings are still raw and visible — easy for young viewers to recognize in themselves.

**Do:**
- Let her feelings be big and real, never played for comic relief only
- Show the moment she finds the right word for a feeling as a genuine small victory
- Keep her rabbit in frame — it's her tell for when she's overwhelmed (she grips it tighter)

**Don't:**
- Never make her meltdowns the joke of the episode
- Never resolve her feelings instantly — naming a feeling takes a beat, not a snap
- Never lose the rabbit or change her outfit between episodes

**Locked visual-prompt paragraph (copy verbatim into every image generation featuring Nell):**
> A 5-year-old girl with two low pigtails tied with red ribbons, warm brown skin, wearing a purple polka-dot dress, holding a well-loved stuffed rabbit. Flat, rounded cutout animation style, soft shapes, warm daytime lighting.
