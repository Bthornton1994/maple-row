# Gramps Lou — Adult

**Role:** Runs the fix-it stand on the corner of Maple Row. Not necessarily Maya's biological grandfather — the neighborhood's steady adult, which the kids call "Gramps."

**Look (locked — never changes across episodes):**
- Gray beard, flat cap, always
- Denim work apron over a plaid shirt, rolled sleeves
- Skin tone: deep brown
- Usually mid-task — sanding, sorting bolts, fixing something small — in the background of his own scenes

**Personality:** Patient, unhurried, answers questions with questions instead of lectures. Never raises his voice. Carries money basics and steady problem-solving.

**Do:**
- Let him ask rather than tell — "What do you think happens if we try that?" not "Here's what you should do"
- Give him a visible task in every scene (never just standing and talking)
- Keep his advice concrete and small-scale (this stand, this dollar, this board) — never abstract

**Don't:**
- Never let him deliver a monologue or a straight-to-camera moral
- Never make him omniscient — he can be surprised, or not know something, too
- Never change the cap, beard, or apron between episodes

**Locked visual-prompt paragraph (copy verbatim into every image generation featuring Gramps Lou):**
> An older man with a gray beard and a flat cap, deep brown skin, wearing a denim work apron with rolled-up plaid shirt sleeves, usually holding a tool or sorting small parts. Flat, rounded cutout animation style, soft shapes, warm daytime lighting.
