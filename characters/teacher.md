# Ms. Rivera — Teacher

**Role:** Maya's teacher at Ridgeline Elementary. A lighter recurring role than Gramps Lou by design — school-set episodes only. Not a second steady grown-up: she is on the clock with a room full of other kids, and that is what makes her a different kind of adult.

**Look (locked — never changes across episodes):**
- Dark brown hair in one high, round bun, always — a single top bump, so she reads instantly against Maya's two puffs and Nell's two low pigtails
- Rust-orange cardigan over a cream shirt, charcoal trousers, flat shoes — no glasses (those are Theo's)
- Skin tone: warm tan
- Tall and vertical where Gramps Lou is broad and low; almost always mid-circulation — crossing the room, crouching down to desk height, or holding a clipboard against one hip

**Personality:** Warm, brisk, genuinely busy. She gives one child her whole attention for a short, real moment, then has to move — twenty other kids and a clock. Answers with a short question and a next step, not a speech. Carries responsibility and safety at school, and states a rule as a plain thing everyone already knows, calmly, never as a warning.

**Do:**
- Give her something to do and somewhere to be in every scene — crossing the room, crouching to a desk, squaring a stack of paper — she is never a talking head in a static wide
- Let her answer with one short question and a concrete next step with a time on it ("Can it be on my desk tomorrow morning?") — Gramps Lou has all afternoon to wonder with a kid, she has the next two minutes, and that limit is the character, not coldness
- Let her be the last to know and fine about it — she didn't see it happen, and she hears "I didn't do it" or "I don't get it" only because Maya says so, out loud, first

**Don't:**
- Never let her deliver the lesson, the moral, or the explanation that does Maya's thinking for her
- Never turn a kid's admission into a ceremony — no pausing the room, no "how brave," no classmate reacting; a confused kid and an undone worksheet are ordinary classroom traffic and she takes them in the same unsurprised breath she'd take a pencil request
- Never change the bun, the cardigan, or her color set between episodes
