# Episode Brief — EP01: The Broken Cup

**Skill pillar:** 01 / Telling the truth — kid-plain: "Say what really happened, even when it's scary"

**Logline:** Maya breaks Gramps Lou's favorite cup at the fix-it stand, says it fell by itself, and then has to watch him start fixing a shelf that was never broken.

**Hook (first 15 seconds):** Frame one is the broken cup — a blue-striped mug in four pieces on the concrete, one piece against Maya's untied shoe, Maya still up on the crate with her hand out toward the shelf. A saw keeps going out of frame; he didn't hear it. Her first two words are "Oh no."

**Conflict:** The cup is broken on the floor and Maya is the only one who was in the room — nobody saw it, nobody is going to find out, and Gramps Lou asks one plain question: "What happened?"

**Wrong solution (the failed try):** "It just fell. All by itself." She lies because she was reaching for the cup to bring him his tea — a big-kid job — and dropping it means she isn't the careful helper anymore. Nobody catches her. That is what makes it fail: believing her, Gramps Lou decides the high shelf must be sloping and starts building a lip for a shelf that was fine all along.

**Right solution (the new try):** With the drill bit touching good wood, Maya says "Wait. Don't fix the shelf" and tells him she dropped it. He thanks her, says he's a little mad about the cup and not at her, rubs his pencil marks off the shelf, and asks what they do about it. Because she said what really happened, she knows where the missing handle went — she was on the crate, it went *that* way — and finds it under the bench. She proposes the repair herself, off a rule she watched him use on a cracked chair rung an hour earlier: glue does the sticking, band does the holding. He corrects the material, not the method — the chair took wood glue, the cup needs its own.

**Skill named in kid language:** "Telling the truth was hard for one minute, but the lie was hard all day." — Maya, Scene 6. Gramps Lou nods once and adds nothing.

**20-second practice for the viewer:** Pick one small true thing to say out loud today, even if it's a little embarrassing. Maya gives two phrases a kid can borrow — "that was me," "I forgot" — in 40 words / 0:22, on the crate with the mended cup in her hands.

**Characters in this episode:** Maya (7) and Gramps Lou — both to their locked sheets in `/characters/`. No one-off side character; no Theo, no Nell. The conflict only works if Maya is genuinely the only one who was in the room.

**Locations used:** Gramps Lou's fix-it stand only — one plate, worked in three areas (front counter, high shelf, workbench end). The second-plate allowance goes unused on purpose: the middle of the episode depends on Maya being able to glance up at that shelf.

**Target runtime:** 6 min (honest estimate 6:38 — 585 spoken words at 110 wpm plus 1:19 of held silence; trim list in the script takes it to 6:19) · **Complexity:** S
