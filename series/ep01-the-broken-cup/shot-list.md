# Shot List — EP01: The Broken Cup

12–20 key frames maximum. Each frame = one AI image generation, built from the locked character prompts in `/characters/`.

**20 key frames — the collapsed budget published in `script.md`, at the cap and not over it.** Shot # is 1:1 with the beat # in `storyboard.md`: same count, same order, same scene keys (the scene key is carried in the Shot # cell so the two files diff row by row). The three slow push-ins (shots 4, 8, 20) are scale-ups of frames that already exist and cost **zero** new generations — they are not listed as separate shots.

**One location plate for the whole episode:** Gramps Lou's fix-it stand (BIBLE.md plate 3 of 4), worked in three areas — front counter under the high shelf, the high shelf itself, the workbench end. No second plate, no new backgrounds, no incidental people anywhere in it. The high shelf runs the whole length of the stand above both working areas, so the dust ring stays in frame from every area.

**How to read the Notes column.** Every row carries the same three-part continuity contract — **Cup** / **Shoe** / **Lou's task** — so any frame can be generated out of order without drifting. Anything after those three is frame-specific.
