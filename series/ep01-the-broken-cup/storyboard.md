Camera options (per BIBLE.md visual rules): **static wide** or **slow push-in** only. No handheld, no fast cuts.

**Push-in accounting.** Exactly three, all marked above and all in the script: beat 4 (the pause), beat 8 (time passing), beat 20 (onto the seam). Each is a scale-up of a frame that already exists, so the storyboard's 20 beats are still 20 key frames — see the collapsed frame budget published in `script.md` and carried into `shot-list.md`.

**Standing continuity across all 20 beats.** One plate, no second background and no incidental people in it. Maya's and Gramps Lou's locked visual-prompt paragraphs from `/characters/` are copied verbatim into every generation; only pose and action change, including across the morning-to-afternoon jump. One hero prop in four states: whole (never seen) / four pieces with the handle missing (beats 1–13) / five pieces laid out (beats 14–16) / mended with visible seams (beats 17–20). The dust ring on the high shelf is a flat circle and reads in every beat up to 12. Gramps Lou has a visible physical task in every beat he is in.
